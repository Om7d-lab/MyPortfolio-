Open Sign

A website made to measure for the local establishment — then set again, precisely, for the next.

Reference No. OS·01  ·  Working Title  ·  First Edition

IThe Commission
Commissioned for

Small local establishments — cafés, barbers, gyms, tutors — and newly founded businesses with no presence online.

The matter addressed

Many have no website, or one that fails on a phone. Hours, address, and offering go unfound — and customers are quietly lost.

The value returned

A composed, trustworthy presence that new customers can find with ease — without the expense of a large studio.

IIThe First Reference

One establishment, finished to a high standard

Version one is a single demonstration house: a small, phone-considered site of a few pages — a measured welcome, the offering, a short gallery, and the means to make contact. Nothing superfluous. Clear enough that an owner sees their own place within it.

IIISpecifications
  • IThe HomeThe name, the nature of the place, and a considered welcome.
  • IIThe OfferingA clear account of the services, or the menu.
  • IIIThe GalleryA short, well-chosen set of images.
  • IVThe ContactHours, address, and a map — plainly presented.
  • VConsidered for the handResolved first for the phone, where it will most be seen.
IVDeliberately Omitted

Held back by design, to keep this reference pure:

accounts & logins online payment reservations a database a complex backend
VProvenance & Future Editions

Made once, as a master

To dress it for a given establishment, only the images, the words, and the finish are changed. One master, many editions — a café, then a barber — each set by hand, for a modest fee.

Made once · Set many times
